# Zero-Spend Policy

Personal spend target: **$0.00**.

## Allowed

- local/open-source tools
- existing operator subscriptions already owned for general use
- verified free tiers
- verified hackathon credits/tokens
- Airwallex sandbox

## Blocked without explicit operator approval

- PAYG fallback
- new paid subscription
- paid cloud activation
- card/deposit requirement
- production financial account funding
- real-money transfer
- resource that may silently continue billing after credits

## Credit rule

A promised credit is not a credit.

Treat credits/tokens as available only after they are:
1. visible,
2. usable,
3. bounded,
4. understood well enough to avoid personal spend fallback.

## Model preference

Prefer local/free development such as Ollama.

Competition LLM access is optional and only becomes usable when free hackathon access is actually verified.
